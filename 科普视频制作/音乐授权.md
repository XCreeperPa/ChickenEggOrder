## 使用场景

### English Original

The video is an original Chinese educational video for Bilibili about formal proof and proof assistants, using Lean 4 as the main example.

The video uses the classic “chicken or egg first?” question as an accessible starting point. It explains how an intuitive philosophical question can be translated into a formal system, written as Lean code, and checked by a proof assistant. It will also briefly show an AI-assisted proof workflow, where an agent helps write a proof while Lean verifies whether the proof is correct.

The video is intended for a general Chinese-speaking audience interested in mathematics, philosophy, programming, formal verification, and AI. It may also mention older philosophical discussions about causality and origin, including Aristotle.

The music will be used as background music only. It will not be redistributed, uploaded separately, or included in any downloadable package. It will only be synchronized with the finished video.

The video will use original Manim animations and the project's own Lean code. It will not reuse 3Blue1Brown's video footage, animations, branding, or narration.

The video will be uploaded to Bilibili and may receive revenue through Bilibili's normal creator programs or platform rewards. It is not a sponsored video, advertisement, paid course, or commercial product.

Agent 帮助书写证明，而 Lean 验证证明是否正确。
